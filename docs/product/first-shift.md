# Your first shift

DashPilot records delivery work you enter and route samples your device captures. It does not
import orders or earnings from another app. No account, target or financial setup is required.

## A short welcome

The four welcome pages introduce the app, tracking work, understanding money and local-first
privacy. Use **Next** and **Back**, or **Skip** to start exploring immediately. Skipping and finishing
both mark the welcome as seen. An established install with recorded shifts is not interrupted.

To revisit it, open the Settings gear in Home, Work or Money, then **Welcome to DashPilot** under
About. **Close** returns to Settings. No permissions are requested by the welcome.

## Start on Home

1. Tap **Start Shift** when you begin work. The app opens Work with your active controls.
2. If you want route recording, use its location explanation and permission control. Only When In
   Use access is requested. A shift can exist without a recorded route.
3. Add a delivery or an offer containing several deliveries. Record Arrived at Pickup, Picked Up
   and Delivered for the appropriate delivery, when it is safe to stop.
4. Use **Park Vehicle** when working on foot. Route recording stops, but working time continues.
   Use **Resume Driving** when leaving. **Pause Shift** is for a break and stops both working time
   and recording; resolve deliveries in progress before pausing.
5. Resolve each delivery before ending the shift. Open its History detail to record shift gross
   earnings or correct a mistake. Delivery earnings are independent of the shift amount.

The [shift workflow](shift-workflow.md) and [delivery lifecycle](delivery-lifecycle.md) explain
stacked offers, optional pickup assistance, Undo and historical corrections.

## Review your records

Home shows completed shifts from the current Monday-through-Sunday week. An active shift joins
those figures only after it ends. Work contains History and Older Weeks. Money lets you select a
week, enter expenses, explore [Insights](insights.md) and open reports. Settings is available from
each workspace toolbar.

Missing earnings are not zero. Recorded mileage may be partial. Fuel estimates are separate from
recorded expenses. Read [earnings and coverage](earnings-and-metrics.md) before interpreting a rate
or net subtotal.

## Optional defaults

Settings can hold a vehicle, fuel assumptions and a **Target hourly earnings** benchmark. A target
applies to shifts started after it is saved. It is optional and never predicts what a shift will pay.
Amounts use USD; there is no currency selector or conversion. See [Settings](settings.md).

## Keep control of your data

Records are local. [Export](history-export.md) is explicit, user initiated, and is not a supported
restore backup. Review [privacy](../architecture/privacy.md) and
[limitations](../reference/limitations.md) before relying on route capture or keeping your only copy
of important records in the app.
